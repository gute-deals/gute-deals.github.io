Who the guides are for

  • For parents and gift-givers who want to pick a set and make sure it suits the person.
  • For beginners who want to know how to read the details on the box.
  • For collectors and anyone who wants to organise, display and care for their models.

How we work

The guides are general, contain no prices and no advertising, and say openly that we have not tested anything ourselves. Details of individual sets come from the manufacturer's product page and are shown with a date.

Errors or requests? You will find contact details in the legal notice of Wirkstatt, linked at the bottom of every page.
