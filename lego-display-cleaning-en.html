Displaying and cleaning LEGO sets: care tips

A finished model should look good for a long time. With the right location and a little care, colours and surfaces stay in shape.

The tips on this page are general and have not been tested by us. What counts are always the details on the box and on the manufacturer's website.

Deutsche Fassung

The right location

Put finished models on a stable, level surface without strong vibration. Avoid direct sunlight: plastic can darken or yellow over time, and light-coloured bricks show it first. Strong heat can deform parts, so a spot next to a radiator is unsuitable.

Keeping dust away

Dust can be removed with a soft, dry brush. A display case or a cover made of transparent material keeps it off. A surface that is wiped regularly also helps.

Cleaning loose bricks

Loose bricks without stickers, electronics or mechanics can be washed in lukewarm water with a little mild washing-up liquid. Do not use hot water, harsh cleaners or a dishwasher. Let the bricks air dry before using them.

Stickers, electronics and built models

For parts with stickers, motors or battery compartments, avoid water and only wipe them dry. Better not to dip built models in water; dust them carefully instead.

Lighting

If you light a model, choose a lamp that gives off little heat and does not shine on the same part all the time.

Checklist

  • ☐ Location without direct sunlight and without a radiator
  • ☐ Stable, level surface
  • ☐ Dust protection or regular dusting
  • ☐ Loose bricks washed only lukewarm and mild
  • ☐ Stickers and electronics only wiped dry

Frequently asked questions

Can bricks go in the dishwasher?

This page advises against it because heat can deform parts. The care instructions of the manufacturer are what counts.

Do white bricks turn yellow over time?

Plastic can darken, especially in strong light. How fast it happens depends on the light and the room. Avoiding direct sunlight can reduce it.

How do I clean a finished model?

With a soft, dry brush or a cloth, carefully and from top to bottom. You do not have to take anything apart.
